Summary

  • TFT Buddy has no account and no server of its own. We do not ask for your name, email, or other contact details.
  • Your comps and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app may show ads from Google AdMob on its own screens. AdMob may use a device identifier and other device data. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can say no.
  • The app uses Google Firebase to count how its features are used (for example, "a comp was created") and to report crashes. It does not send your comps, and it does not use your advertising ID for this.

1. Who we are

TFT Buddy ("the app") is made by Gyeongdae Kim, an individual developer publishing as KKD App Studio ("we"). We are responsible (the controller) for the data described in this policy. Contact: kkd.appstudio+buddy@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data on your device only. It is not sent to us.

  • Comps you save (name, champions, positions, stars, items).
  • Settings: the app language you chose, the last set you viewed, and whether you finished the first-run guide.
  • Game data files the app downloads (see section 4).

When you delete the app, this data is deleted.

3. Clipboard and sharing

  • The app reads the clipboard only when you tap paste, to find a team code. It does not read the clipboard in the background.
  • When you copy or share a team code, the app puts the code on the clipboard or opens your device's share sheet. You choose where it goes.

4. Game data downloads

The app downloads public game data (champion, trait, and item data and icons) from our data server, which is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. These requests contain no personal information from the app. Like any web request, they include your IP address and basic device and app information, which Cloudflare may process as our hosting provider. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app may show ads from Google AdMob on its own screens. Ads are never shown in the Picture-in-Picture (PiP) window or on top of the game. Ad content is limited to the "Teen" rating.

To show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • a device advertising identifier: on iOS, only if you allow tracking; on Android, the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language),
  • ad interactions (views and taps).

Personalized ads are used only where you allow them:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before ads. You can change your choice later in the app: Settings › Ad privacy settings.
  • On iOS, the app asks once whether it may track you. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google › Ads).

If you do not allow personalization, you still see ads, but they are not based on your activity in other apps. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To learn which features people use and to fix problems, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

  • What the app sends: which feature you used, with short fixed values. For example: you created a comp (by hand or from a team code), saved, duplicated, or deleted a comp, copied or shared a team code, turned on the overlay, changed the set, or finished the first-run guide. Comp names, champions, team codes, and anything you type are not sent.
  • What Firebase collects automatically: an app instance ID created for each installation, app opens and time in the app, app version, operating system, device model, language, and approximate location (country, region, or city) derived from your IP address (Google Analytics does not store your IP address).
  • Not collected: your advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android) and, on iOS, the identifier for vendor (IDFV).
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, usage statistics are turned on only if you agree in Google's consent form (storing information on your device and measuring content performance). You can change your choice later in Settings › Ad privacy settings. Elsewhere, usage statistics are on.
  • Usage statistics are not used for advertising.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

  • When the app crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a crash report: where in the app's code the problem happened, the time, app version, operating system, device model, and an installation ID for crash reports.
  • We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs.

See the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing and transfer

We do not sell your personal information. For ads, Google receives device identifiers and ad interaction data directly from the app and uses them for its own advertising purposes, as described in section 5 and Google's policies. For usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) and game data (Cloudflare), these companies process data on our behalf as service providers.

Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. For transfers from the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google and Cloudflare rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and standard contractual clauses.

8. Retention

We keep no personal information on a server of our own. Data on your device stays until you delete it or the app. Firebase deletes user-level usage statistics after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days; aggregated statistics (for example, daily counts) may be kept. Google (ads) and Cloudflare keep data according to their own policies.

9. Legal bases (EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

  • Ads and usage statistics: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given in Google's consent form. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Ad privacy settings. Withdrawing does not affect processing before it.
  • Crash reports and game data downloads: our legitimate interest in providing a stable, working app and fixing errors (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object by contacting us.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your choices and rights

We do not hold your name or contact details. Usage statistics and crash reports are linked only to a random installation ID, so we cannot find them by your name or email. If you delete and reinstall the app, it gets a new ID. You can control advertising data and, where consent is required, usage statistics with the settings in sections 5 and 6.

In the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. For questions, contact us at kkd.appstudio+buddy@gmail.com.

12. Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's security. Game data is downloaded over HTTPS, and the app checks the files before it uses them.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the effective date above.

어떤 기능이 쓰이는지 알고 문제를 고치기 위해 Google Analytics for Firebase와 Firebase Crashlytics를 씁니다.

사용 통계(Google Analytics for Firebase)

  • 앱이 보내는 것: 어떤 기능을 썼는지와 정해 둔 짧은 값. 예: 덱을 만듦(직접 또는 덱 코드로), 저장·복제·삭제함, 덱 코드를 복사하거나 공유함, 오버레이를 켬, 세트를 바꿈, 처음 안내를 끝까지 봄. 덱 이름, 챔피언, 덱 코드, 입력한 글은 보내지 않습니다.

  • 동의: 유럽경제지역, 영국, 스위스에서는 Google 동의 창에서 동의한 경우(기기에 정보 저장, 콘텐츠 성과 측정)에만 사용 통계를 켭니다. 앱의 설정 › 광고 개인정보 설정에서 나중에 바꿀 수 있습니다. 그 밖의 지역에서는 켜져 있습니다.
  • 사용 통계는 광고에 쓰지 않습니다.

꺼짐 보고(Firebase Crashlytics)
