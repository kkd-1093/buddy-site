TFT Buddy

A companion app for Teamfight Tactics (TFT) Mobile that keeps your chosen team comp board visible in a small Picture-in-Picture (PiP) window while you play.

In development (pre-release) · iOS first, Android planned

What it does

  • You paste a team code (the official in-game team planner code) or pick a comp you made.
  • You place the champions on a board and add star levels and items yourself.
  • You start PiP with a button. A small window shows your final board layout on top of the game.
  • You can hide the window and open it only when you need it.

Who it is for

Beginner and intermediate mobile players who want to follow a comp without switching apps or using split screen during a game.

What it does NOT do

  • It does not read the game screen, game memory, or game data.
  • It does not track opponents or the lobby.
  • It does not give live or dynamic advice. The PiP window shows only a static board that the user prepared before the game.
  • It does not call the Riot Games API. Game assets (champion, item, and trait icons) come from Riot's public static data.
  • No ads are shown inside the PiP window or on top of the game.

How it works

  1. Copy a team code from the game or a community site.
  2. Paste it into the app. The app shows the champions in the comp.
  3. Drag the champions onto the board. Add stars and items if you want.
  4. Tap the PiP button. The board stays on top of TFT Mobile.

Monetization

Free to use. One ad card may appear in the comp list on the app's home screen. Ads never appear in the PiP window, over the game, or in the comp editor.

Privacy

No account, and your comps stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy (개인정보 처리방침).
